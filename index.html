Those are general-purpose LLMs. Design Floor is 14 specialized agents that each have a specific role, collaborate in sequence, and produce structured, cross-referenced artifacts. Scout's research becomes Palette's creative constraint. Flow's UX states become Blueprint's spec inventory. The agents talk to each other — not just to you.
Your data is stored in your own Supabase account with row-level security; other users can't access it. LLM calls go through a Cloudflare Worker, meaning your brief and outputs are never cached or shared.
Design Floor produces text-based artifacts: research, specs, code, and structured documents. It doesn't generate images, render Figma files directly, or replace user testing with real humans. Think of it as a senior design team writing the specification, not an image generator.
