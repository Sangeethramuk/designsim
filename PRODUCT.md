# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Solo product designers working alone or in small teams who need the output of a full design team — research, UI, specs, code — without the headcount. They arrive with a brief and a deadline. They're confident in what they want but stretched on time. The emotional goal on landing: *this tool has my back*.

## Product Purpose

Design Floor gives solo designers a 14-agent AI design team that runs a full sprint from a single brief — producing research, UI variations, UX flows, component specs, production code, and a quality-gated critique in one pass. It's the accelerant a one-person design function uses to ship at team-scale.

## Brand Personality

Creative, rigorous, surprising. The personality in three words: **designer-made, seriously fast**. Tone: direct, confident, a little bit delightful — never chatty, never corporate.

The pixel art studio is a creative choice, not a game; it signals that this was built by someone who thinks in systems and has a strong point of view. The sophistication is in the concept, not in ornamentation.

## Anti-references

- **Overly playful / gamey**: The pixel art concept must not read as a toy or hobby project. No comic fonts outside the logo, no chiptune metaphors in copy, no "XP" or "level up" gamification language.
- **Generic SaaS template**: The gradient-hero / feature-grid / social-proof-ticker pattern. Design Floor is made by a designer and should look it.
- **AI tool cliché**: Dark purple / indigo palette, glowing neural network motifs, "powered by AI" badge language.

## Design Principles

1. **Pixel art is the signature, not the surface.** The RPG studio concept is the unexpected creative bet. Let it appear deliberately and sparingly — in the hero canvas, in the logo — so it reads as a confident design choice rather than a theme that overruns the product.
2. **Confidence through restraint.** A solo designer needs to trust the tool instantly. Clean hierarchy, minimal decoration, no hedging copy. The absence of noise is the signal.
3. **Show the output, not the process.** Visitors care what they'll walk out with. Artifact-first content over architecture diagrams.
4. **Design-world identity.** This is made by a designer for designers. Typography, colour, and micro-interactions should feel peer-to-peer, not vendor-to-customer.
5. **Speed as the value prop.** Every section should reinforce the core promise: one brief, everything you need.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA as minimum. All body text must meet 4.5:1 contrast on its background. Prefers-reduced-motion respected for all animations. Press Start 2P used only for decorative labels (zone IDs, logo) — never for body copy or interactive elements.
